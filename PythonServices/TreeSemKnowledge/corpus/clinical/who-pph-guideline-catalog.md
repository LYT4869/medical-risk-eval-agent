# WHO consolidated postpartum haemorrhage guideline: catalog overview

## Source status

The World Health Organization, the International Federation of Gynecology and Obstetrics, and the International Confederation of Midwives published the first consolidated guideline on prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of postpartum haemorrhage on 5 October 2025. The 116-page publication has ISBN 978-92-4-011563-7 and brings together 51 evidence-based recommendations.

## Scope and audience

The guideline covers pregnancy, childbirth, and the immediate postpartum period in health-care settings. Its primary audience is health professionals and people responsible for national or local protocols, including midwives, nurses, general medical practitioners, obstetricians, programme managers, health ministries, and training institutions.

## Retrieval boundary

This checked-in catalog overview supports source discovery and questions about the document's scope. It does not reproduce clinical recommendations. Answers about a specific recommendation require an approved local snapshot of the full guideline and must cite that snapshot's page or section.
